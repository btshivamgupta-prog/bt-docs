# Adhoc Job

Adhoc Job is the non-recurring visit path (`admin.visit`). It creates a specific piece of service work with customer/site context, schedule, team, line items, notes/attachments, and closure information.

## Lifecycle

1. Create the job with valid customer/site and required work data.
2. Schedule and assign the appropriate team/resources.
3. Execute: record arrival/work/time, forms, tools, notes, and attachments as applicable.
4. Close through the supported completion workflow; generated reports and financial follow-up should reference the finalized visit.

Changing an assigned technician, date, asset, or line item has calendar, availability, history, and reporting impact. Preserve audit history and surface conflicts instead of overwriting them.
