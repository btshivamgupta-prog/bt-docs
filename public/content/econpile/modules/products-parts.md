# Products & Parts

Products & Parts is the catalog and inventory foundation for sales, visits, rentals, assets, and delivery. Products and parts have separate routes and UI, but share recurring concerns: identity/barcode, pricing, stock, serialisation, warranty, movement history, and line-item usage.

Never update stock as an incidental form side effect. Inventory changes must have an auditable business reason and movement trail. Use the dedicated numeric control for monetary/quantity inputs and validate business limits server-side.
