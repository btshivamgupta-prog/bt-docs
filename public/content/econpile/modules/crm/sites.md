# Sites

Sites are the operational locations at which customer work and assets exist. The menu route is `admin.property` (the application uses “property” in code).

## Why sites matter

Site selection supplies the location context for visits, assets, deliveries, and public QR submissions. It should resolve from its customer relationship; do not allow a site from another customer to be attached silently.

When changing site fields, test address/contact display in customer detail, job creation, asset detail, and documents.
