# Introduction to Plasma FSM

Welcome to the **Plasma FSM** documentation. Plasma is a powerful Field Service Management (FSM) tool designed to streamline operations, manage inventory, and track service workflows.

---

## What is Plasma?

Plasma FSM provides comprehensive tools for:

- **Field Service Management** — manage visits, jobs, and field technicians
- **Inventory & Stock Management** — track product stock across the supply chain
- **Serialized Product Tracking** — trace individual serialized items through their lifecycle
- **Stock Adjustments & Audit** — maintain accurate inventory with full audit trails
